How it works

Three steps to a smaller GPU bill.

  1. 01

    Find the waste

    We set Throttle up on your stack and price every token: dollars per million with a 95% confidence interval, from your traffic shape and what your GPUs cost per hour. Repeat checks learn how much your setup swings run to run, so every later saving is judged against that noise.

  2. 02

    Cut the bill

    We try the changes that stop you paying for wasted GPU time: engine flags, batching, GPU count, quantization, engine version. A change only counts if it beats your noise, and Throttle flags OUTPUT CHANGED when answer lengths or token-cap hits move.

  3. 03

    Prove every dollar

    Each kept change comes with a savings statement from throttle savings, line by line. After the 2 months, you pay a share of the savings we verify together. No savings, no bill.

Why proof matters

Faster is not cheaper.

On Qwen2.5-72B and rented AMD MI300X GPUs, going from 1 GPU to 2 gave 1.56x the throughput, and cost $2.14 instead of $1.67 per million output tokens: 28% more per token, because you pay for both GPUs.

On the same 72B, vLLM’s on-the-fly FP8 looked 47% cheaper, but the output changed. On the 32B it answered “!!!!”. Throttle caught both from answer length and token-cap hits. It does not grade answer content, so we read samples with you too.

Same-server runs also swing with nothing changed (the write-up). That is why only savings that clear your measured noise count. Raw checks are on GitHub, and more results are in the Cost Index.

From you

What we need.

  • An endpoint. Your workload on an OpenAI-compatible endpoint (vLLM, SGLang, TGI, Ollama, TensorRT-LLM, NIM or similar) on GPUs you own or rent, or a box with the same model, engine and GPU type.
  • Your GPU price. What your GPUs cost per hour, so tokens and time become dollars.
  • Your traffic shape. A representative prompt sample or a description of what your agents send.
  • Your volume. Monthly production tokens, so savings can be put in dollars.

Throttle runs on your side and only sends requests to your own endpoint. Prompts, responses and keys stay in your environment, and we’re happy to work under your NDA.

FAQ

Questions.

Why free for 2 months?

We want real results on real workloads. Two months is enough to price your baseline, cut what matters and prove each saving against your own noise. Each pilot is hands-on, so we keep the group small.

What happens after?

You keep every config change and every statement. If we saved you money, you pay a share of the savings we verified together. If we didn’t, you pay nothing, and the open-source CLI stays free.

We already benchmark our serving stack.

Most benchmarks report throughput from one run, and throughput is not your bill. The pilot prices every config in dollars per million tokens, learns your run-to-run noise first, and only counts a saving that clears it. Sometimes it shows a “win” was noise.

Does Throttle check answer quality?

No. OUTPUT CHANGED looks at answer length and token-cap hits, not content. For quality we review answer samples with you before any change is kept.

Do you need production access?

Not for testing. A staging endpoint or a dedicated box with the same model, engine and GPU type is enough. The savings statement needs your production token volume.
