How it works

Three steps, on your stack.

  1. 01

    Measure

    We set Throttle up and measure your dollars per million tokens with a 95% confidence interval, using your traffic shape and what your GPUs cost per hour. Repeat checks measure how much your setup swings run to run. Every later change is judged against that noise bound.

  2. 02

    Fix

    We try changes that waste less GPU time: engine flags, batching, GPU count, quantization, engine version. A change only counts if it beats your noise, and Throttle flags OUTPUT CHANGED when answer lengths or token-cap hits move.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Each kept change comes with a savings statement from throttle savings, line by line. After the 2 months, you pay a small share of what we save. No savings, no bill.

Why measure

Faster is not cheaper.

On Qwen2.5-72B and rented AMD MI300X GPUs, going from 1 GPU to 2 gave 1.56x the throughput, and cost $2.14 instead of $1.67 per million output tokens: 28% more per token, because you pay for both GPUs.

On the same 72B, vLLM’s on-the-fly FP8 looked 47% cheaper, but the output changed. On the 32B it answered “!!!!”. Throttle caught both from answer length and token-cap hits. It does not grade answer content, so we read samples with you too.

Same-server runs also swing with nothing changed (the write-up). That is why only changes that clear your measured noise count. Raw checks are on GitHub, and more results are in the Cost Index.

From you

What we need.

  • An endpoint. Your workload on an OpenAI-compatible endpoint (vLLM, SGLang, Ollama or similar) on GPUs you own or rent, or a box with the same model, engine and GPU type.
  • Your GPU price. What your GPUs cost per hour, so tokens and time become dollars.
  • Your traffic shape. A representative prompt sample or a description of what your agents send.
  • Your volume. Monthly production tokens, so savings can be put in dollars.

Throttle runs on your side and only sends requests to your own endpoint. Prompts, responses and keys stay in your environment, and we’re happy to work under your NDA.

FAQ

Questions.

Why free for 2 months?

We want real results on real workloads. Two months is enough to measure your baseline, find the changes that matter and prove each one against your own noise. Each pilot is hands-on, so we keep the group small.

What happens after?

You keep every config change and every statement. If we saved you money, you pay a small share of what we save. If we didn’t, you pay nothing, and the open-source CLI stays free.

We already benchmark our serving stack.

Most benchmarks report throughput from one run. The pilot reports dollars per million tokens with confidence intervals, measures your run-to-run noise first, and only counts a change that clears it. Sometimes it shows a “win” was noise.

Does Throttle check answer quality?

No. OUTPUT CHANGED looks at answer length and token-cap hits, not content. For quality we review answer samples with you before any change is kept.

Do you need production access?

Not for testing. A staging endpoint or a dedicated box with the same model, engine and GPU type is enough. The savings statement needs your production token volume.
