Privacy

What this website collects, where it goes, and how to have it deleted.

Kushagra KanaujiaFounder, ThrottleUpdated Nov 9, 2026

This page is about this website. For what the Throttle software does with your data, see Security.

The signup form

When you press Start now, the form sends your work email and the plan you picked, plus your company and your monthly AI API spend range if you filled them in. A small function on this site sends them as one email through Resend, an email service, to Kush’s inbox at kush@throttle-pro.com. The function does not save them anywhere else.

If you pick Starter or Team, you then pay on Stripe’s checkout page. Stripe handles your card; Throttle never sees or stores card numbers. Kush receives your email, the plan and whether the payment went through.

If that does not go through, your email app opens with the same details filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send.

What we do with it

Kush reads it and writes back to set you up. Your details are not sold.

Tracking

This site’s code runs no analytics or ad scripts and sets no cookies. It is hosted on Vercel. Every page loads a font from Google Fonts, and the footer loads launch badges from Product Hunt, Fazier and DANG!. Your browser fetches those files from them, so they see your IP address and browser, as with any web request.

Deleting your details

Write to kush@throttle-pro.com from the address you signed up with and ask. Kush deletes your signup email and replies when it is done.
