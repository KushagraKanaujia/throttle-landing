FAQ

Questions.

Is Throttle free?

The CLI is free and open source under MIT: pipx install throttle-pro. Throttle Cloud starts with $10 of free credits, no card. Enterprise teams can start with a free 2-month pilot. See pricing.

Does Throttle check whether answers are correct?

No. OUTPUT CHANGED looks at answer length and how often responses hit the token cap. That catches broken output like the FP8 runs above, but it does not read or grade answer content.

Why not just benchmark throughput?

Because throughput is not your bill. Two GPUs can be faster and still cost more per token. Throttle puts the GPU price into the number and checks it against your run-to-run noise, so a lucky run can’t pass as a saving.

Does it work with my stack?

If you serve models on GPUs you own or rent behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, yes. It works with vLLM, SGLang, TGI, Ollama, LMDeploy, llama.cpp, TensorRT-LLM, NIM and similar servers.

Do you need production access?

No. A staging endpoint or a box with the same model, engine and GPU type is enough to find and prove savings.

Where does my data go?

Throttle runs on your side and only sends requests to your own endpoint. Prompts, responses and keys stay in your environment. We’re happy to work under your NDA.
