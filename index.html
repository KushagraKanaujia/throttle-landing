What you get

A number you can defend. A verdict you can act on.

One command against your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint. It sends requests, times them, and turns GPU hours into dollars per million tokens. It never changes your server.

A noise bound before any verdict

FAQ

Questions.

Is Throttle free?

The CLI is open source under MIT: pipx install throttle-pro. The pilot is free for 2 months. After that, you pay a small share of what we save, and nothing if we save nothing.

Does Throttle check whether answers are correct?

No. OUTPUT CHANGED looks at answer length and how often responses hit the token cap. That catches broken output like the FP8 runs above, but it does not read or grade answer content.

Why not just benchmark throughput?

Because throughput is not cost. Two GPUs can be faster and still cost more per token. Throttle puts the GPU price into the number and measures your run-to-run noise, so a single noisy run can’t pass as a saving.

Do you need production access for the pilot?

No. A staging endpoint or a box with the same model, engine and GPU type is enough to measure and test. To put savings in dollars we need your monthly token volume.

Where does my data go?

Throttle runs on your side and only sends requests to your own endpoint. Prompts, responses and keys stay in your environment. We’re happy to work under your NDA.
