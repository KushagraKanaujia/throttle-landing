Every AI job, tested for the way home.

  1. 01

    Set up once.

    Point your API base URL at Throttle and add the GitHub Action. Throttle sees every call your app makes to OpenAI or Anthropic. Nothing else changes.

  2. 02

    It watches every change.

    When your team adds or changes an AI feature, Throttle notices in the pull request and picks the job up. No ticket, no project.

  3. 03

    Tests before it moves. Falls back if not.

    Other models, closed or open, answer the same requests in the background, your own model among them. If one does the job as well for less, you approve and the job moves. If it errors or gives a broken answer, that request goes straight back to the API.

  4. 04

    Keeps every miss.

    Every fallback and every lost test is saved in your cloud as a lesson for your model. Training your model on them is coming

Straight answers.

Something else? Ask Kush.

Does it need access to my code?

It uses a GitHub Action that reads where your code calls an AI API. That is how it finds your AI jobs and comments on pull requests. It runs in your own CI, and your prompts and data stay in your cloud.

Where does my model run?

In your own cloud, on GPUs you control. Your prompts and answers never come to us, and Throttle never stores your API keys.

What if my model gets something wrong?

If your model errors, times out or sends a broken answer (cut off, empty, bad JSON or a bad tool call), that request goes to OpenAI or Anthropic automatically, so your users still get an answer. The miss is saved as a lesson. Training your model on lessons is coming

Do I have to approve every move?

Yes. A job moves only after it passes its tests and you approve it. You can pin any job to the API or undo a move, and every decision is logged. Letting passing jobs move on their own is coming

What does it cost?

Starter is $49.99 a month for one app. Team is $99.99 a month for all your apps. Large companies get a custom plan. No cut of your savings. See the plans.

Which models does it test?

Any model behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, closed or open: a hosted API model with your key, or an open model you serve with vLLM, SGLang, Ollama or LMDeploy. Every one runs the same tests on the same requests your app sent to OpenAI or Anthropic. The savings math is built for models you own; for cheaper API models it is coming

I already use a gateway or an eval tool. Do I still need this?

Keep them. A gateway carries your requests and an eval tool runs the tests you write. Throttle answers a different question: which model should do each job this week, on your traffic, and it moves the job when the answer changes. See how it compares.

We are setting up our first teams now. Every number on this page comes from our own runs on rented GPUs and our own machines, with the raw data on GitHub.
