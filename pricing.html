How savings are measured

From your bills. Not from guesses.

Before the move, we look at what each kind of request cost you on the API.

After the move, we add up what that same traffic costs now. That is your GPU bill, plus any calls that fell back to the API.

The gap between the two is your savings. Both numbers come from your own logs and bills, so you can check every one.

FAQ

Questions.

What does the assessment cost?

Nothing. It runs on your machines, on your own traffic, and you keep the report either way.

What do you charge after that?

A share of the money you save each month, measured from your own traffic and GPU bill. We agree on the share with you before anything moves.

What if there are no savings?

Then there is no bill. If a month saves nothing, you pay nothing for that month.

Is the code open source?

Yes. The Throttle CLI is open source under the MIT license. You can read every line on GitHub.
