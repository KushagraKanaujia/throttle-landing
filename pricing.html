A free look first. Then one flat fee.

How it goes

  1. 01 · Free look

    See what could move. Nothing moves.

    Add the GitHub Action and point your API base URL at Throttle. Throttle runs on your own traffic and shows what could move, what should stay, and what it would save.

    $0

  2. 02 · You decide

    Read the tests. Pick the jobs.

    Every job comes with its tests in plain words. Approve the moves you want, or walk away and keep the report.

    Your call

  3. 03 · Keep it

    One flat fee each month.

    Throttle moves what passes and you approve, falls back when it must, and saves every miss as a lesson for your model.

    Always under your savings

You know the price before you start.

Throttle doesn’t take a cut of your savings. The fee is set from the size of your AI bill, so it stays well under what you save. As more jobs move to your model, the extra savings are yours.

The fee covers everything. Your GPUs are the only other cost.

Your model runs in your cloud, on GPUs you pay for. Throttle counts that cost before it shows savings, so “saved” means saved.

  • A note on every AI change. Cost, test results and the decision, right in the pull request.
  • Tests before every move, on your real requests.
  • Fallback always on. Errors and broken answers go to the API, not to your users.
  • Every miss saved as a lesson, ready to train your model on. Training is coming
  • Money you can see, job by job, every month.
  • Full control. Approve, pin, undo, and a log of every decision.

About the money.

Something else? Ask Kush.

What does the free look cost?

Nothing. Throttle runs on your own traffic and shows what could move and what it would save. Nothing moves, and you keep the report either way.

What do you charge after that?

One flat monthly fee, set from the size of your AI API bill. It is always smaller than what Throttle saves you.

Why aren’t prices on this page?

They depend on the size of your bill, and we are setting them with our first teams. Ask and we will give you a straight number.

Who pays for the GPUs?

You do, in your own cloud. Throttle takes that cost off before it shows savings, so the number you see is what you actually keep.

Can I go back to the API?

Yes, any time. Pin one job, or every job, back to the API. Your app keeps working the whole time.

Is the code open source?

Yes. The Throttle code is on GitHub under the MIT license.
