01Why a flat fee

You know the price before you start.

Throttle doesn’t take a cut of your savings. The fee is set from the size of your AI bill, so it stays well under what you save. As your model learns and handles more jobs, the extra savings are yours.

02What the fee covers

Everything. Your GPUs are the only other cost.

Your model runs in your cloud, on GPUs you pay for. The meter counts that cost before it shows savings, so “saved” means saved.

  • A note on every AI change. Cost, test results and the decision, right in the pull request.
  • Tests before every move, on your real requests.
  • Fallback always on. A miss goes to the API, not to your users.
  • Weekly training on misses, so your model handles more jobs over time.
  • The meter and a monthly receipt, job by job.
  • Full control. Approve, pin, undo, and a log of every decision.

03Questions

About the money.

Something else? Ask Kush.

What does the free look cost?

Nothing. Throttle runs on your own traffic and shows what could move and what it would save. Nothing moves, and you keep the report either way.

What do you charge after that?

One flat monthly fee, set from the size of your AI API bill. It is always smaller than what Throttle saves you.

Why aren’t prices on this page?

They depend on the size of your bill, and we are setting them with our first teams. Ask and we will give you a straight number.

Who pays for the GPUs?

You do, in your own cloud. The meter takes that cost off before it shows savings, so the number you see is what you actually keep.

Can I go back to the API?

Yes, any time. Pin one job, or every job, back to the API. Your app keeps working the whole time.

Is the code open source?

Yes. The Throttle code is on GitHub under the MIT license.
